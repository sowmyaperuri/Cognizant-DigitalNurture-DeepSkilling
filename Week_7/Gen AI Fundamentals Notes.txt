Gen AI Fundamentals Notes
-------------------------

Generative AI creates new content instead of only analyzing existing data.

Features of Generative AI:
Generates human-like text
Creates images
Generates code
Summarizes documents
Answers questions
Translates languages
Creates audio and videos

A Large Language Model (LLM) is an AI model trained on massive amounts of text to understand and generate human language
Examples: GPT, Gemini, Claude

How LLMs Work:
Large Text Data
        ↓
Model Training
        ↓
Learns Patterns
        ↓
User Prompt
        ↓
Generated Response

A prompt is the instruction or question given to a Generative AI model.

Prompt Engineering is the process of writing clear and effective prompts to obtain better AI responses.
Example:
Poor Prompt: Explain Java.
Better Prompt: Explain Java in 100 words with three advantages and one real-world example.

Types of Prompts:
Zero-shot Prompting: No example is provided.
One-shot Prompting: One example is given.
Few-shot Prompting: Two or more examples are provided before asking the model to perform the task.

Foundation Models are large pre-trained AI models that can be adapted for many different tasks using prompting or fine-tuning.
Examples: GPT, Gemini, Llama

A token is the smallest unit of text processed by an LLM.
A sentence may be divided into several tokens depending on the tokenizer.

Hallucination occurs when an AI model generates incorrect or fabricated information while presenting it as if it were true.

Fine-tuning is the process of training a pre-trained AI model on a specific dataset to improve its performance for a particular task.

Responsible AI means developing and using AI systems in a safe, fair, transparent, and ethical manner.
